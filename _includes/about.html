Who am I?

I'm Sebastian Southwell, a DevOps Engineer at the UK Home Office and a First-Class MComp Computer Science graduate from the University of Sheffield. I care about building systems that are actually reliable, not just working — with a particular interest in proof-of-concept design, cyber security, and emerging technologies.


Outside the day job, I'm a 3-time HackSheffield prize winner (3rd place at HackSheffield 7, 3rd place plus a vendor prize at HackSheffield 8, a vendor prize at HackSheffield 9), and spent my degree taking on technical and leadership roles across university societies — from Technical Systems Officer to Lead Imaging Systems Engineer on a UAV competition team. My most recent project was a master's-level group research effort on reducing LLM hallucinations, where I led the system architecture — awarded a Distinction.
