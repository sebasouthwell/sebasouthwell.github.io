description: >
  A two-semester, masters-level group research project (COM4520) with an
  external client, government body Building Digital UK, tackling
  hallucinations in multi-modal LLMs over real government documents. I led
  the system architecture (see the
  pre-processing pipeline diagram): documents land in S3, get split into
  multimodal elements, and are embedded two ways — locally via CLIP
  (concatenated text+image vectors in ChromaDB) and via cloud Bedrock Titan
  multimodal embeddings — while an LLM-driven graph transformer extracts
  entities and relationships into a Neo4j knowledge graph. At query time,
  structured graph traversal is combined with vector similarity search
  (hybrid retrieval), so answers are cross-checked against explicit
  entity-relationship evidence rather than embeddings alone. The
  architecture is provider-agnostic by design — a model catalogue swaps
  between OpenAI, Bedrock (Claude, Llama 3, Mistral), Gemini, xAI and local
  Ollama models (DeepSeek R1, LLaVA, Qwen2.5) — and every configuration was
  scored with BERTScore and ROUGE-L across 275 benchmark runs. Delivered
  with a pytest suite, GitHub Actions CI/CD with Discord build
  notifications, and secure credential handling throughout (74%).
