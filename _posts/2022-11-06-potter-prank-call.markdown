description: >
  A 24-hour hackathon project matching a user's face to the Harry Potter
  character they most resemble, then prank-calling them in character via
  Twilio. I helped build the training dataset — scraping candidate images
  per character with Selenium, then filtering, background-removing and
  normalising them to reduce colour bias — before training a classifier on
  Teachable Machine. The site (HTML/JavaScript on a Node.js server) takes
  an uploaded photo or webcam capture, runs it against the model, and
  triggers a Twilio call scripted around the matched character. Even this
  early on, our hackathon strategy was the same one we'd repeat in later
  years: reach for as many different technologies as the 24 hours would
  allow, treating it as a learning opportunity first. Won Best Hack 3rd
  place at HackSheffield 7.
