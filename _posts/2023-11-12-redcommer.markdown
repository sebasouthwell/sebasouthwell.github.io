description: >
  A 24-hour hackathon build that turns Reddit comment history into
  AI-generated imagery: pick a handful of subreddits, and a sampled set of
  their 2011-era comments (pre-processed down from ~15 million to ~2
  million) is pulled from a MongoDB Atlas store, summarised and reformatted
  into an image prompt by ChatGPT, then rendered with DALL-E. I worked on
  the backend data server — the real engine behind the Streamlit frontend —
  wiring up and debugging three separate third-party APIs with Postman,
  each needing its own handler. As with our other hackathon entries, the
  strategy going in was to deliberately combine as many different
  technologies as possible in the 24 hours, using the time pressure as a
  learning opportunity rather than just a race to ship. Won Best Hack 3rd
  place and MLH Best Use of MongoDB Atlas at HackSheffield 8.
