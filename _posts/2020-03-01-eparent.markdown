  actively discouraged to maximise marks for complex, self-built behaviour

  tailored revision questions during pre-set study sessions, enforced by
  background program detection that blocks distractions (games, browsers)
  until the session ends. No public release, but I plan to rebuild a
  cleaner, more polished version eventually.
