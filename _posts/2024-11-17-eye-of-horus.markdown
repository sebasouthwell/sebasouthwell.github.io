description: >
  Inspired by Oasis AI's real-time AI-rendered Minecraft, this 24-hour
  hackathon build asked whether the same idea could work for a physical
  device instead of a screen: a Kitten-box handheld wired to a joystick,
  displaying an AI-generated "open world" that responds to navigation
  input. We wrote the analog joystick and image-display firmware directly
  on KittenCode online — working around limited firmware control and no
  native image support, which meant designing a custom image format and a
  manual drawing approach — while Databricks, using OpenAI's DALL-E and
  LangChain, generated the environment images from user prompts behind a
  Streamlit frontend. Like all our hackathon builds, the strategy in a
  24-hour competition was to deliberately reach for as many different
  technologies as possible, treating the time pressure as a fast learning
  opportunity rather than just a race to ship. Won [MLH] Best AI Project
  with Databricks Open Source at hackSheffield 9.
