description: >
  A real-client Android app (COM4510 team project) built for Regent Court
  Locksmiths, letting landlords manage properties and raise repair requests
  with their contractors. Built in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose, it supports
  full property/repair CRUD with photo attachments, an embedded Google Maps
  picker (with a custom pointer-gesture fix to stop the map fighting with
  its own bottom-sheet modal), auto-composed repair request emails with
  attachments, and phone number auto-detection via Android's
  TELEPHONY_SUBSCRIPTION_SERVICE. Requirements were gathered through
  customer personas and QOC (Quality-Options-Criteria) decision tables to
  weigh UX trade-offs as a team of four. Delivered using a proper agile
  workflow: Trello Kanban, git feature branches with peer-reviewed merge
  requests, and a GitLab CI/CD pipeline wired into Discord for build
  notifications.
